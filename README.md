# Mil_3100
​3100: A narrative-driven action-drama game set in a darker late 19th-century Switzerland and France.
# Mil_3100: Phase 1 - Ashes of Vrin & The Shadows of Paris

> **Setting:** Late 19th-Century Switzerland and France (1888–1894)  
> **Genre:** Narrative-Driven Action-Drama / Stealth & Mechanical Mechanics  
> **Protagonist:** Milad (Mil) - A genius mechanical inventor with a physical impairment (limp).

---

## 📖 Phase 1 Overview (Levels 1 to 100)

### Section 1: Childhood Trauma in Vrin (Levels 1–10)
* **The Origin:** Growing up in the isolated village of Vrin, Switzerland.
* **The Tragedy:** A tragic fire destroys Mil's home, separating him from his past and instilling a lifelong distrust of his surroundings.
* **The Impairment:** Mil sustains a permanent leg injury, shaping his reliance on custom mechanical supports, canes, and strategic stealth rather than brute strength.

### Section 2: Awakening & Military Service in France (Levels 11–40)
* **Conscription & Warfare:** Enlisting in the French military border division.
* **Engineering Solutions:** Designing mechanical traps, custom weapon stabilization systems, and squad tactics to compensate for physical limitations.
* **The Limping Officer:** Earning recognition among officers through strategic intellect and mechanical innovations.

### Section 3: The Journey for Truth (Levels 41–70)
* **The Discovery:** Intercepted logs reveal the fire in Vrin was not accidental.
* **Desertion & Confrontation:** Leaving the military to investigate the ruins of his past.
* **The Betrayal:** Uncovering dark secrets involving local figures and the truth behind his family's desertion.

### Section 4: The Underground Empire & Clara (Levels 71–90)
* **Paris Catacombs:** Establishing a hidden workshop underneath the streets of Paris.
* **Meeting Clara:** Forming an alliance with Clara, an influential figure in the Parisian underground.
* **The Shadow Network:** Building mechanical devices, covert gear, and an underground influence ring.

### Section 5: The Grand Betrayal & Falling (Levels 91–100)
* **The Ambush:** Government raid on the central workshop following a critical betrayal.
* **The Climax:** Clara reveals her true allegiance to the French intelligence unit.
* **Escape to the Unknown:** Severely injured, Mil escapes Paris via a cargo ship bound for North Africa, marking the end of Phase 1.

---

## 🛠️ Core Gameplay Mechanics
* **Limp & Weight Management:** Movement speed and stability dynamically affect aiming precision and stealth noise.
* **Mechanical Crafting:** Building custom contraptions, lockpicks, pneumatic gear, and exoskeletal leg supports.
* **Environmental Traps & Stealth:** Utilizing line-of-sight, noise dampening, and mechanical traps to outsmart superior forces.
